# SCASP Android Guide
1. **Install**: copy the APK to the phone, allow installation from this source, open it.
2. **Log in** with your demo account.
3. **Home** has four buttons: Course catalogue, My registrations, Service requests, Campus services.
4. **Offline**: if there is no internet the app shows the last saved list and says so. Reopen the screen when back online to refresh.
5. **Campus services**: tap a service to open it in your map app. Tap *Show my position* to see your coordinates; the phone asks permission only then. If you say no, maps still work.
6. **Rotate** the phone any time; your list stays.

## Permission explanation
Location is used only to display your position when you press *Show my position*. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

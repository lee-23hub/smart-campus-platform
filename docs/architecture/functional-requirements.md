
# 2. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

The following functional requirements describe the operations and services that the Smart Campus Academic Services Platform (SCASP) must provide.

## FR01: User Authentication
The system shall allow students and staff to log in using valid account credentials.

## FR02: Role-Based Access
The system shall provide different access permissions for students, administrative staff and system administrators.

## FR03: User Management
The system shall allow authorised administrators to create, update and manage user accounts.

## FR04: Course Catalogue
The system shall allow students and staff to view available courses, course codes, titles, capacity and status.

## FR05: Course Management
The system shall allow authorised staff to create, update, search, sort and manage courses.

## FR06: Course Registration
The system shall allow students to submit requests to register for available courses.

## FR07: Course Withdrawal
The system shall allow students to withdraw from registered courses.

## FR08: Duplicate Registration Prevention
The system shall prevent students from registering for the same course more than once.

## FR09: Waiting List Management
The system shall automatically place eligible students on a waiting list when a course reaches its registration capacity.

## FR10: Academic Service Requests
The system shall allow students to submit academic service requests, including transcript, clearance and appeal enquiries.

## FR11: Request Processing
The system shall allow authorised staff to prioritise, assign, process and update the status of academic service requests.

## FR12: Announcements
The system shall allow authorised staff to publish announcements for defined audiences and allow students to retrieve relevant announcements.

## FR13: Notifications
The system shall generate notifications for important registration and academic service request events.

## FR14: Reporting
The system shall generate reports showing course capacity, waiting lists, registration records and academic service request workloads.

## FR15: Audit Trail
The system shall record important system activities, including the user responsible, action performed and date and time of the activity.

## FR16: Campus Services
The system shall display information about selected campus services, including their descriptions and geographical locations.
